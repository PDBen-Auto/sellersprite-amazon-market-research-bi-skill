# Licensing and edition policy

This repository currently uses a source-available license. The public edition exposes the documented SellerSprite export workflow, scope rules, deterministic normalization scripts, synthetic examples, and dashboard method while keeping credentials and customer exports out of the package.

## Public edition

Use the package within the rights granted by `LICENSE`. SellerSprite and Amazon remain third-party services; this repository does not grant access to either service or their data.

## Reserved material

The following remain separately controlled:

- SellerSprite credentials, customer exports, supplier records, and private research datasets;
- private adapters, operational collection recipes, and commercial integrations;
- managed research, custom category rules, and support services.

## Official release status

Modified forks are not official PDBen-Auto releases unless the manifest and provenance signature verify. This policy does not grant rights beyond `LICENSE` and is not legal advice.
