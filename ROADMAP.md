# Roadmap

## Near term

- Add synthetic fixtures for more categories, marketplaces, and ambiguous Listing attributes.
- Improve detailed-export coverage diagnostics and missing-history explanations.
- Add reusable dashboard sections for price, review, and compatibility dimensions.

## Later

- Add adapters for additional lawful marketplace research exports.
- Add benchmark datasets for scope classification and parent-ASIN aggregation quality.

## Non-goals

- Treating keyword results as complete category TAM without a stated baseline.
- Presenting SellerSprite estimates as Amazon settlement data.
- Storing credentials, hidden telemetry, or private customer exports.
